## What is Joomni Studio?

[Joomni](https://joomni.com) is Nepal's all-in-one, no-code platform for websites, online stores and POS. Studio is the part of it made for designers. It has four pieces:

1. **The same builder merchants use.** You author themes in Joomni's own visual page builder, with the identical sections, settings and renderers a merchant sees. Nothing can look right in Studio and break on a real shop.
2. **A free sandbox store.** A sandbox is a real Joomni store flagged as a dev store: free forever, never billed, no checkout and not indexed. It comes seeded with demo products, prices and images, so your layouts have real content to render.
3. **Review before anything goes live.** Submitting freezes a snapshot of your theme. Automated checks run instantly, then a human reviews design quality and originality.
4. **Install copies, it never links.** When a merchant installs your theme it is copied into their store. Later edits on your side never touch a live shop, so a mistake of yours can never break someone's business.

There is nothing to install and nothing to host. No repo, no build step, no deployment.

## Why build themes for Joomni

- **70% revenue share** on every sale, once paid themes open (see [Earnings](#earnings-and-revenue-share)).
- **Real use.** Your work runs on real Nepali businesses, not just in a portfolio screenshot.
- **90+ built-in sections** to design with, the same library merchants get: heroes, product grids, galleries, menus, booking forms and more.

## How it works

From sign-up to published, five steps.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1. Create your builder account** | A Joomni account and a short application: who you are, and any work they can look at. A portfolio helps but is not required. Students with nothing published yet are welcome. |
| **2. Get a free sandbox** | You get a sandbox store the moment you apply, before approval. One while your application is pending, five once you are approved. |
| **3. Design your theme** | Build in the visual page builder. Sections, colors, fonts, layouts, header and footer, and Liquid if you want dynamic markup. Preview live as you go. |
| **4. Submit for review** | Submitting takes a snapshot. Automated checks run instantly and tell you exactly what to fix. If they pass, a human reviews design quality and originality. |
| **5. Get published** | Approved themes go live in the Theme Store and on your public profile at `joomni.com/b/your-handle`. Every merchant on Joomni can find and install them. |

Review takes **5 business days** (2 at Verified tier). If a theme is rejected, you get written feedback and can fix and resubmit as many times as you like. A rejection is not permanent and does not affect your account.

## What you can and cannot ship

Your theme runs on other people's shops, next to their customers' checkout, so the rules on code are strict and are enforced automatically when you submit.

### Allowed

- All 90+ built-in sections: heroes, product grids, galleries, menus, booking forms
- Full control of colors, typography, spacing and layout
- Liquid sections for custom markup with live store data
- Images uploaded to the Joomni media library

### Not allowed

- Custom JavaScript, or any section that runs raw code
- Custom-coded header and footer sections
- Images hot-linked from other websites
- Someone else's design, template or copy

**Why no JavaScript?** A theme runs on a stranger's storefront, in the same browser session as their customers' logins and payments. Third-party code cannot be fully reviewed for what it might do later, so none is allowed. Liquid gives you dynamic content without that risk.

## Earnings and revenue share

Builders keep **70%** of every sale. Joomni takes 30%.

> **Paid themes are not switched on yet.** For now the theme catalog is free while the payout system is built, so early themes earn installs, ratings and reputation rather than cash. Your 70% share applies from the day paid themes open.

Publishing free themes now is a good way to build a public track record. A builder with a few well-rated themes and a few hundred installs has something far more useful to show a client than a CV, and is first in line when paid themes open.

**Ownership:** you own your theme and license it to Joomni to host and sell. Merchants who installed it keep a perpetual license to keep using it, even if you later remove it.

## Before you submit: a checklist

Run through this in your sandbox before you hit submit.

- [ ] Original design and copy. Nothing taken from another theme or website.
- [ ] No custom JavaScript and no custom-coded header or footer.
- [ ] All images uploaded to the Joomni media library, none hot-linked.
- [ ] Previewed on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- [ ] Every page has clear headings, readable text sizes and enough color contrast.
- [ ] Demo content looks realistic, with no placeholder text left behind.
- [ ] Buttons and menus link to real pages.
- [ ] Consistent fonts and colors across all pages.
- [ ] A clear theme name and a short description of who it is for.
- [ ] Read the [Builder Terms](https://joomni.com/joomni-studio/terms).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to know how to code?
No. The page builder is entirely visual. If you want more control, Liquid is available. It is a simple templating language, closer to writing HTML than programming, and plenty of approved themes use none of it.

### What does it cost?
Nothing. Sandbox stores are free and never billed, and you do not need a paid Joomni plan to build or publish.

### Do I need to open a store to become a builder?
No. A builder account is its own signup, with no store name or subdomain needed. If you already run a shop on Joomni, you can use the same account.

### How much do I earn?
Builders keep 70% of every sale once paid themes open. Until then the catalog is free.

### How long does review take?
Five business days. Builders at Verified tier get two.

### What if my theme is rejected?
You get written feedback explaining why, and you can fix and resubmit as many times as you like.

### Who owns the theme?
You do. You license it to Joomni to host and sell.

### Will my updates change stores that already installed my theme?
No. Installing copies the theme into the merchant's own store. Nothing you publish afterwards touches a live shop.

### Can I use JavaScript?
No. Custom JavaScript is not allowed. Use Liquid sections for dynamic content with live store data.
